OUR STORY

Three generations of bringing the heat

A LEGACY OF FIRE & FLAVOR

In 1985, Giuseppe Vinchenzo arrived in New York with nothing but a dream and his grandmother's secret spicy sauce recipe. What started as a tiny storefront in Little Italy has grown into a beloved institution known across the city for one thing: the most incredible spicy pizzas you'll ever taste.

Giuseppe believed that great pizza should excite all your senses - especially your taste buds. He spent years perfecting his signature spicy sauce, combining rare peppers from Calabria with locally sourced ingredients. The result? A pizza experience that's bold, unforgettable, and genuinely hot.

Today, the Vinchenzo family continues Giuseppe's legacy. His son Marco runs the kitchen, while granddaughter Sofia manages the front of house. Together, they ensure that every pizza that leaves our ovens carries the same passion and fire that Giuseppe brought to America nearly 40 years ago.
